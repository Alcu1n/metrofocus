# MetroFocus original sound design

All six WAV files are original deterministic syntheses generated by the included
`generate_audio.py` using Python's standard library. No third-party recordings,
models, samples, or sound libraries were used. They may be shipped with this app.

Regenerate and validate:

```sh
python3 MetroFocus/Resources/Audio/generate_audio.py
```

- `metro.wav`: 12-second loop, low filtered noise, 48/72 Hz rumble and soft periodic rail joints.
- `rain.wav`: 12-second loop, broadband filtered rain with gently modulated density.
- `air.wav`: 12-second loop, softer low-pass air with a slow breathing envelope.
- `departure.wav` / `arrival.wav`: original ascending/descending three-note station chimes.
- `punch.wav`: short synthesized mechanical impact and rebound.

Format: mono, 22,050 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM. Assets peak at -12.04 dBFS before the
app's additional volume attenuation. Noise filters run a warm-up period over the
same samples; tones and modulation have integer periods in the loop. The measured
loop seam is below the 99th percentile of ordinary adjacent sample differences.
Exact measurements are recorded in `audio-validation.json`.

Signal validation establishes nonzero audio, headroom and digital continuity.
Comfort, perceived looping, actual loudspeaker volume, interruption handling, and
headphone route changes still need listening and physical-device verification.
